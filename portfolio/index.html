How prioritization works

Environmental exposure contributes up to 30 points; inventory 25; sourcing 20; lead time 15; and criticality 10. Scores below 25 are Low, below 50 Moderate, below 75 High, and 75 or above Critical.

Missing inventory produces an incomplete assessment in the full app. Zero consumption is handled explicitly.

What this sample cannot tell you

Scores are operational heuristics, not damage probabilities. Stockout dates are estimates. Supplier capacity is unknown until confirmed. Complaint counts reflect reporting activity, not fleet-adjusted failure rates.

Real vehicle defects are never attributed to the synthetic suppliers.

What is available now?

This static sample works entirely in your browser. The full application is implemented and tested locally and in GitHub Actions. Its public server deployment is pending; the optional Snowflake integration has not been verified against a warehouse account.

Where does the full app obtain public data?

Environmental data adapters use USGS and Open-Meteo. Automotive adapters use NHTSA. No external data is fetched by this sample website.
